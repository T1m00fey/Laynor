Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 April 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1 This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Timofey Aleksandrovich Yudin, acting as a private individual developer without formal business registration (“Operator”, “we”, “us”, “our”), processes personal data of users (“you”, “User”) of the mobile application “ReadBox” (“Application”).

1.2 For the purposes of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of the Russian Federation, the Operator is a personal-data controller; for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”), the Operator is a data controller.

1.3 By installing, accessing, or using the Application, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, you must stop using the Application and uninstall it.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data provided during registration

  • e-mail address;
  • password (stored only in hashed form using bcrypt or an equivalent secure hashing method);
  • user name (nickname).

2.2 Data generated while using the Application

  • texts of posts, articles, comments, and other content you publish;
  • images and other files you upload;
  • technical and device-related data, including IP address, device model, operating system version, application version, language, and general diagnostic information;
  • crash reports collected through Firebase Crashlytics;
  • aggregated analytics and usage events collected through Google Analytics for Firebase;
  • push notification token (APNs/FCM) and notification permission status.

2.3 Subscription-related data

If you purchase or use the Read+ subscription through Apple App Store, the Application may locally access limited subscription status information made available through Apple StoreKit, such as whether you currently have an active subscription or entitlement to paid content.

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit your bank card details or other payment credentials. Payment processing is performed by Apple.

The Application does not send subscription receipts, transaction identifiers, or purchase history to the Operator’s own servers or to Firebase for subscription verification. Subscription status is checked locally on your device through Apple StoreKit.

2.4 Special categories of data

We do not intentionally request or process special categories of personal data (for example, data concerning health, religion, political opinions, or similar sensitive data). If you voluntarily publish such data within the Application, you do so at your own discretion and risk.

2.5 Children

The Application is not intended for persons under 16 years of age (or under 13 where COPPA or similar rules apply). By registering, you confirm that you meet the applicable minimum age requirement or have valid parental or legal guardian consent where required by law.

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the core functionality of the Application, including account registration, authentication, content publication, synchronisation, and access to paid content where a valid Read+ subscription exists;
  • Legitimate interests — to ensure security, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot issues, improve performance, and optimize the Application;
  • Legal obligations — to comply with applicable laws, lawful requests from public authorities, and other legal requirements;
  • Consent — where consent is required, including for optional marketing push notifications or newsletters.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • registration, authentication, and account management;
  • publication, display, storage, backup, and synchronisation of user content;
  • enabling user interaction features, including posts, articles, and comments;
  • sending push notifications, including:
    • transactional notifications, such as system messages, replies, and technical alerts;
    • marketing notifications, news, or offers, where you have opted in;
  • analytics, diagnostics, A/B testing, service improvement, and performance monitoring;
  • ensuring the security and integrity of the Application;
  • complying with applicable laws and protecting our legal rights;
  • enabling and controlling access to content available under the Read+ subscription by checking subscription status locally on the user’s device through Apple StoreKit.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 User-generated and application data are primarily stored in Google Firebase (region: europe-west), except where certain functions depend on Apple infrastructure, such as App Store subscription processing.

5.2 We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including:

  • encryption in transit using TLS;
  • encryption at rest provided by our infrastructure providers;
  • access restriction on a need-to-know basis;
  • two-factor authentication on administrative accounts where available;
  • monitoring and diagnostic tools to detect service issues and abuse.

5.3 Retention period:

  • account and related application data are stored for as long as your account remains active;
  • backup copies may be retained for up to 30 days after deletion or deactivation, unless a longer period is required by law or for legitimate dispute-resolution, security, or legal-compliance purposes;
  • passwords are never stored in plain text;
  • push notification tokens are deleted or become inactive within a reasonable period after you disable notifications or delete your account.

5.4 Since subscription status is checked locally through StoreKit and is not stored on our own backend for verification purposes, we do not maintain a separate server-side subscription history database for such checks.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

We may share personal data only to the extent necessary with the following categories of recipients:

  • Google LLC / Google Firebase — for hosting, cloud database, crash reporting, analytics, and related infrastructure services;
  • Apple — for App Store distribution, in-app subscription processing, subscription status handling within Apple’s ecosystem, and payment-related operations;
  • e-mail service providers — if and when used for newsletters or support-related communication;
  • competent public authorities, courts, regulators, or law-enforcement bodies — where required by applicable law or lawful request.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.
